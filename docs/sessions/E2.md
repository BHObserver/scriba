**Status:** done. `bun run check` (358 tests, 79 new) and `bun run build` pass. No live Meta calls, $0 spent. Started by merging `scriba-mvp` (B1–B3b) into `track-engine`, a fast-forward.

## What's done (`src/lib/insights/`, pure functions, money in integer poisha)

- **`book.ts`** `prepareBook(input)` puts confirmed records in khata order (business date, page capture time, line). It follows merged parties to their target (cycle-safe) and keeps one reading per page (see Decisions). Every figure is a `Figure = { poisha, recordIds }`, for drill-down.
- **`day.ts`** `daySummary(book, date)`:
  - Sales (cash and credit), repayments, money in, and each money-out type.
  - **The drawer cash** = morning cash + cash sales + repayments − cash purchases − supplier payments − expenses, with `parts` for the receipt.
  - `close` holds counted cash, the gap and `changedSinceClose`.
  - `dayCloseSnapshot(day, counted)` is the row E3 stores in `day_closes`.
- **`accounts.ts`** `partyAccount`, `partyAccounts`, `duesList(book, "customer" | "supplier", asOf)`:
  - Running balance, last payment, the oldest unpaid credit by FIFO, `unpaidRecordIds` (the lines a reminder shows).
  - Weekly balances and risks. Dues are sorted by rank, then days, then balance.
- **`trends.ts`**: `salesByDay`, `weekComparison` (7 days against the 7 before), `topItems` (spellings grouped) and `weekdayPattern`.
- **`note.ts`** `scribaNote`: up to 3 typed sentences, in order: unchecked page(s), sales split (or "no sales" on a day with lines), the riskiest customer, the week trend.
- **`text.ts`**:
  - `noteText(s, i18n)` and `riskText(r, kind, i18n)` fill templates from the dictionary.
  - `bnPossessive` adds the Bangla case ending: দিদির, ভাইয়ের, করিমের, শরতের, Rahim-এর.
- **`today.ts`** `todayInsights(book, asOf, pages)`: everything Today needs in one call, including `isNewShop`.
- **`testing.ts`** ports D3's sample shop (`proto/shop.js`). The tests reproduce the prototype's drawer (৳৪,৩২০), sales (৳৬,২৯০, of which ৳১,৩৪০ on credit), its three risks and its Bangla note word for word.

## Decisions

- **One reading per page.** "Fresh" analysis makes a new run of the same page, whose lines can be confirmed too. Only the run confirmed last counts; the rest are listed in `supersededRecordIds`.
- **Morning cash:**
  - A closed day uses what it was closed with (`closed`).
  - Otherwise, the last close's counted cash, if no day since then had records (`counted`; shut days carry over).
  - Otherwise, the profile's usual cash (`usual`).
- **Opening balances** count only as a party's starting point, when one comes before any credit or payment of that party. Later ones repeat the balance and are ignored. Closing balances, subtotals and `adjustment` lines (direction unknown, kept in `notCounted`) are in no figure.
- **Risk rules** are D3's, with these choices:
  - **no_payment** (30 days for customers, 21 for suppliers) needs both the oldest unpaid credit and the last payment to be that old, so paid-off-then-new credit isn't flagged.
  - **big_credit**: "recent" is the last 7 days including today. "Usual" is the upper median of at least 3 older credits; the credit must be 3× that and at least ৳১,০০০.
  - **growing** means the balance rose 3 weeks running.
  - No risk is raised while nothing is owed.
- **Comparisons are only made when the book reaches back far enough.** The week trend needs records from 13 days back. The weekday pattern uses 4–12 whole weeks ending yesterday.
- **Note copy is in the i18n dictionary** (`note.*`, `risk.*`, bn and en). The engine returns data; `text.ts` inserts the numbers. English keeps the name as written.
