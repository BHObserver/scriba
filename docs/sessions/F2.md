7. **The page finder gave up on his pages** (flow-2, 4 Nov: "পাতাটা খুঁজে পাইনি" on both pages, all four corners dragged by hand; branch `f2-video-fixes`).
   - **Cause:** C2's finder takes the largest bright, colourless region as the page. His pages never lie alone on a dark table. In flow-2 a narrow notebook page lies on a red notebook across two pale yellow files, on white sheets that run off the frame, and at Otsu's split all of it is one "paper" region, not page-shaped, so `none`.    - **Fix:** the page is also looked for as four straight edges (`src/components/scan/edges.ts`). A Hough transform over the full circle keeps which side is brighter, so every line knows where its paper is. Lines are refitted to their own edge pixels, and one line per side makes a quad. A quad's score is the share of its outline that is an edge with the paper's own level inside. So a side that runs on over a lit table doesn't count, and one where the page lies on a white sheet (no edge, paper inside) isn't held against it. Near-equal quads are the same sheet with or without the curled roll of his pads or a notebook's binding tape, and the bigger one wins, so live and still agree. `detectPage` runs both finders: the region one's quad is kept when they agree within 2% of the frame, or when its own outline scores as well. Otherwise the edge quad is snapped at full size with a threshold per side (file on one side, table on another) and a tight band, so it can't jump to a tape's outer edge.
   - **Measured** with `bun scripts/measure-corners.ts private-test-data/corners/truth.json` ($0, `--draw <dir>` for corner zooms) on his 13 photos: f2 P1–P2, 2 Nov P1–P5 (camera originals), V01, V02, 1–4.jpeg.
