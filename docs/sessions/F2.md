3. **A part's picture sometimes showed the wrong lines** (his report, 4 Nov; branch `f2-part-snippet`). The pages he saw it on were cleared before it could be looked at there (see below), so it was measured on P1–P5's stored readings (the app's own reads of 2 Nov, plain prompt 6 and prompt 7).
   - **Cause:** the model's line boxes are often off by up to a third or half of a line, either way. Measured against the ink on the page, the median box is 43/1000 high, and the ink starts 16 below the box top and ends 7 above its bottom. But for 10% of lines the ink runs past one edge, by up to about 35. Snippets fit the box exactly and fade everything outside it. So 17 of 194 line strips and 6 of 60 part pictures cut and faded part of their own writing: P2's day-sales মোট ১৫০ and ২৯০, P4's আড়ং visit's last item and নাসির's মোট, P3's ফ্রেশ visit's last line, P5's ইউনিলিভার line. None of the readings had a part whose box held another part's lines.
   - **Fix:** a line's strip reaches 0.3 of its height past the box above and below (`lineWindow`, `src/components/kit/strip-geometry.ts`). A part's picture reaches 0.6 of a line past its first and last line, but never past halfway to another part's line above or below (`blockRegion(rows, page)` in `ask-model.ts`). Re-measured: line cuts 17 → 1, part cuts 6 → 0, and no other part's writing inside. The tinted parts on the page seal use the same regions.
   - **Still possible:** when the model's boxes for the last line of one part and the first line of the next both lean toward each other (P4's নাসির মোট ২৪০ then রিনা), splitting the gap halfway still clips a little. Snapping boxes to the ink when a page is read would settle that; not done.
   - **His data was cleared on 4 Nov** to test from the first scan, at his request: `bun scripts/clear-shop.ts --yes` (new; a dry run without `--yes`). It removed 93 pages (sample and his), 20 photos, 539 khata lines, 19 customers and suppliers, 5 closed days and the sample shop profile. It kept the login and his learning switch (on). The app opens its welcome once. The handwriting notes come back as he confirms new pages.

6. **Line pictures showed another line on a long page** (first recording, then his second, `video/phone/flow-2.mp4`, 4 Nov evening, where he said he had to struggle more). On the first page 1, from ফ্রেশের কামাল down, each line's picture showed the line below, so "দিলাম ৮০০", read as ৪০০ and not doubted, was checked against the দুধ line's picture. In flow-2, page 2 got worse further down: "আগের বাকি ২৭৫০" showed "দিলাম ২০০০", and the last cards (মুড়ি, ডাল/গুড়, টক দই) showed blank paper.
   - **Cause:** the model's line boxes drift, and not by a fixed amount. On page 2 (808×2018, about 30 lines) every gap was about a fifth too long: half a line low at the top, five lines low near the bottom, then squeezed. On page 1 the boxes were a full line low in the middle (ফ্রেশের কামাল … ম্যাচ) and right again at the bottom. On the 2 Nov pages (wider, fewer lines) they were within half a line. Fix 3's 0.3 of a line of room can't cover that.
   - **Fix:** the boxes are put on the writing when a page is read (`src/lib/images/ink-rows.ts`, `snap-boxes.ts`, the pipeline's `snapBoxes`). The ink is how much darker each pixel is than the paper around it (pen ink is far darker than the ruled lines and the writing from the back page); his underlines (slanted pen strokes), ruled lines and the page edges are taken out; rows are the peaks of ink across the page, and a row too tall for one line is split where a quarter-strip of the page shows two lines (P3's "দুধ / আটা } হিসাব পরে"). The model's lines are matched to the rows in order by a dynamic programme: close to the model's place at the top, a widening reach lower down, gaps stretched against the model's but steadily, a heading row expected above a titled part, rows like the date and "নতুন মাল:" left unmatched. A line takes its row's height and the whole written width; "= ১৯৫" written under its line joins it. When fewer than 60% of the lines find a row, or anything fails, the model's boxes are kept. About 0.2–0.4 s a page on the server, no model call.
   - **Stored as read:** `extracted_entries` can't be updated (a trigger keeps the model's candidates as they were), so the snapped box is what a new reading stores; the model's own boxes stay in the run's raw output. **Pages read before this keep their drifted boxes**, his two flow-2 pages included; a fresh scan gets the new ones. Every picture of a line reads the stored box: the numbers screen, cards, part pictures, the khata's strips and the handwriting notes' crops.

Snapped: 276/277, his two pages 20/20 and 19/19, nothing cut.** The one left: P3's second reading, where the "Page 3" label above the first line joins its row, so its picture shows the label above আলু ২ কেজি ৭০.
   - **Stress test** (`--drift`): the 2 Nov pages' boxes moved like his 4 Nov pages, then snapped: a line low everywhere, a bulge, a smooth stretch 237/238; squeezed 228/238; page 2's own drift curve 211/238.
