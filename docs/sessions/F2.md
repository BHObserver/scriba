## Fix list

1. **"লেখা এরকমই, রেখে দিন" fails with "রাখা গেল না"** (written total ৳3,650, correct on the page). **Fixed**, commit below. Every answer to a "sum doesn't come out" card (`unbalanced`: "use" or "keep") was refused with 400 since U2 (2 Nov). Its fingerprint was `ackFingerprint(f)` and its id was `off:<finding key>`, and the finding key lists every line's UUID. So the fingerprint was always over the route's 40 characters, and the id went over 120 from three lines up. The tests used ids like `r1`, so they never got near the limits. Now both are hashed like every other question's. What "keep" accepts for the approval rules is still the full `ackFingerprint`. The limits are `MAX_QUESTION_ID`, `MAX_OPTION_ID` and `MAX_FINGERPRINT` in `src/lib/questions/types.ts`, and a test runs real UUID line ids through them. Nothing was saved by the refused answers, so his page needs no repair: after the deploy the same card comes back and "keep" works.

3. **A part's picture sometimes showed the wrong lines** (his report, 4 Nov; branch `f2-part-snippet`). The pages he saw it on were cleared before it could be looked at there (see below), so it was measured on P1–P5's stored readings (the app's own reads of 2 Nov, plain prompt 6 and prompt 7).
   - **Cause:** the model's line boxes are often off by up to a third or half of a line, either way. Measured against the ink on the page, the median box is 43/1000 high, and the ink starts 16 below the box top and ends 7 above its bottom. But for 10% of lines the ink runs past one edge, by up to about 35. Snippets fit the box exactly and fade everything outside it. So 17 of 194 line strips and 6 of 60 part pictures cut and faded part of their own writing: P2's day-sales মোট ১৫০ and ২৯০, P4's আড়ং visit's last item and নাসির's মোট, P3's ফ্রেশ visit's last line, P5's ইউনিলিভার line. None of the readings had a part whose box held another part's lines.
   - **Fix:** a line's strip reaches 0.3 of its height past the box above and below (`lineWindow`, `src/components/kit/strip-geometry.ts`). A part's picture reaches 0.6 of a line past its first and last line, but never past halfway to another part's line above or below (`blockRegion(rows, page)` in `ask-model.ts`). Re-measured: line cuts 17 → 1, part cuts 6 → 0, and no other part's writing inside. The tinted parts on the page seal use the same regions.
   - **Still possible:** when the model's boxes for the last line of one part and the first line of the next both lean toward each other (P4's নাসির মোট ২৪০ then রিনা), splitting the gap halfway still clips a little. Snapping boxes to the ink when a page is read would settle that; not done.
   - **His data was cleared on 4 Nov** to test from the first scan, at his request: `bun scripts/clear-shop.ts --yes` (new; a dry run without `--yes`). It removed 93 pages (sample and his), 20 photos, 539 khata lines, 19 customers and suppliers, 5 closed days and the sample shop profile. It kept the login and his learning switch (on). The app opens its welcome once. The handwriting notes come back as he confirms new pages.
