## Fix list

1. **"লেখা এরকমই, রেখে দিন" fails with "রাখা গেল না"** (written total ৳3,650, correct on the page). **Fixed**, commit below. Every answer to a "sum doesn't come out" card (`unbalanced`: "use" or "keep") was refused with 400 since U2 (2 Nov). Its fingerprint was `ackFingerprint(f)` and its id was `off:<finding key>`, and the finding key lists every line's UUID. So the fingerprint was always over the route's 40 characters, and the id went over 120 from three lines up. The tests used ids like `r1`, so they never got near the limits. Now both are hashed like every other question's. What "keep" accepts for the approval rules is still the full `ackFingerprint`. The limits are `MAX_QUESTION_ID`, `MAX_OPTION_ID` and `MAX_FINGERPRINT` in `src/lib/questions/types.ts`, and a test runs real UUID line ids through them. Nothing was saved by the refused answers, so his page needs no repair: after the deploy the same card comes back and "keep" works.

2. **Numbers first, then the questions** (his feedback, 4 Nov; branch `f2-numbers-first`, merged into scriba-mvp; not deployed when written). He said: (a) sometimes a figure was never put to him, and on the "মিলেছে" stamp he saw a wrong figure he couldn't change; (b) Scriba kept asking "cash or credit?" though a sale with no বাকি written is cash, so it should be a tag on the card he can change; (c) he wants to check *all* the numbers together after the scan, fix the wrong ones, submit, and only then get the other questions, without the whole thing getting long. What was behind it:
   - (a) A "yes" on "এই ৩টা বিক্রি কি সব নগদে?" accepted every sale on that part, figures shown only as small chips (the `block_kind` rule is "lines"), and then the part's stamp showed the total with nothing to tap.
   - (b) On his pages every day-sales line comes back `unknown` (the prompt says never to guess credit), so `blockKindQuestion` asked once per sales part: twice on P1, three times on P2.

   What changed:
   - **The numbers screen** (`ask-numbers.tsx`) replaces the overview as the first screen. Every part, every line's handwriting with the figures read from it under it, each figure a button to the fix sheet. Doubted figures are turmeric (the fix sheet offers Scriba's two or three readings as one tap, so his ৩/৬ in P1's মোট shows "২৬০ / ২৩০"). Under each part: what code worked out, and any sum that doesn't come out. That sum can be settled right there ("৳X ধরুন" / "লেখা এরকমই"), so the deck doesn't come back to numbers. "সব সংখ্যা ঠিক আছে" marks the doubted figures seen (`markChecked`), saves day sales of no kind as cash, takes the photo's day when none is written (shown as a chip at the top, changeable), and accepts every line he saw that needs nothing else.
   - **Cash unless বাকি is written**: `cashByDefault` in `src/lib/ledger/blocks.ts`. Applied when a reading is parsed (`normalizeModelOutput`, which also drops a doubt about the kind) and when a part becomes the day's sales ("what is this part?" or a split). Readings stored before this are saved as cash by the numbers screen. The part's tag "নগদ বিক্রি ▾" opens a sheet to mark any sale বাকি (it asks who took it, since a credit sale needs a name).    - **The deck only asks.** `acceptSeen` (`ask-model.ts`) accepts a line he saw once no question is open on it and its figures haven't changed since, so the quick confirm only comes for lines an answer changed, added or moved (T2). A question put off for later still keeps its lines out of the khata.
   - **No stamp card per part** (one tap less per part). The page seal's parts open their lines to fix a figure ("কোনো সংখ্যা ভুল? অংশটায় চাপ দিয়ে ঠিক করুন।"), and its back arrow goes to the numbers screen. "Fix this page" (T5) also starts on the numbers screen.
