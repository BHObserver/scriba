## What's done (part 1)

- **`src/components/check/ask-model.ts`** (pure, `ask-model.test.ts` on the Pran page with the real engine):
  - `isBlockReading(page)`: a page with blocks gets the ask deck; prompt 4 pages keep the line cards.
  - `deckItems(review, set, acceptances, seen)`: what's still to go through, in order. Item kinds: `question`, `prices` (consecutive `price:<row>` of one block), `line` (a problem no question covers), `rest` (quick confirm) and `stamp`. Done items drop out, so `items[0]` is the current card. `itemKey` gives each item a stable key; `seen` holds the stamp keys already shown.
  - `acceptAnswered(review, question, acc)` after each answer; `acceptRest(review, ids, acc)` for "সব ঠিক আছে".
  - `blockFigures(block, rows)`: the visit or account card (owed before, paid, goods with `amount: null` as a gap, `known`, `now` null while a price is missing), or a total for sales and expenses.
  - `blockRegion(rows)`: the union of the lines' boxes (blocks store none), for the overview's tinted regions and the visit crop.
  - `blockTone`, `blockDone`, `questionCounts`, `coveredLines`.
- **`ask-api.ts`**: `AskApi` = `ReviewApi` + `answer(review, question, fingerprint, answer)` → `{ review, set }`, or `stale` (409), `bad_value` (422) or `failed`. `httpAskApi(runId)` posts to U2's route. `localAskApi(base)` runs `answerQuestion` and `buildQuestions` in memory for the dev board.
- **Strings:** 59 `asks.*` keys in bn and en (the `ask.` prefix belongs to P2's Ask Scriba). They're also in COPY.md Part 5, which lists the ones written here that a Bangla reader hasn't checked.
- IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN: "Answers and acceptance (B7)" and "Ask deck order (B7)".

## Decisions (reasons in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN)

- An answer accepts its lines only when nothing is left to decide on them. Anything else gets the quick confirm or its own line card. Confirmation and approval rules are unchanged.
- **Order: D5's, not the engine's.** The page's date question comes first, then block by block, and the seal comes last. **Back** returns to the overview, because a saved answer has no undo.
- "Later" questions leave the block without a stamp (its card shows the open due). The page screen offers "Answer them now". Confirming sends only accepted lines, so unpriced goods stay unconfirmed (D5).

## Know this

- `reviewOf` (deck fixture) drops `block`; `ask-model.test.ts` sets it on the values.
- An opening balance line needs `amountText` to be approved, like any line. Readings put "আগের ২৮০০" there.
- The engine asks no `meaning` for `day_sales` or `expenses`, and no `block_kind` when the lines are typed. So a typed sales block is just a quick confirm and a stamp.
