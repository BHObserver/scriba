## What's done

- **`src/components/scan/`** (pure TS, tested; no OpenCV):
  - `geometry.ts`: corner order, homography, hull, largest inscribed quad, robust line fit, `refineQuad`, flat size.
  - `detect.ts`: paper score (brightness − ½ colour) → 240 px grid → Otsu → largest region touching ≤ 2 frame sides → hull → quad. Then 3 passes of snapping each side to the real edge (walk in from outside to the threshold crossing, robust fit). Status: `none | cut | far | found | khata`.
  - `markers.ts`: the khata lock. It finds the four hollow 6 mm squares where the outline predicts them, then maps the A5 sheet corners through their centres.
  - `warp.ts`: bilinear homography warp. `scan-core.ts`, `scan.worker.ts` and `scanner.ts`: the worker, with a main-thread fallback.
  - UI: `use-page-finder.ts` (about 10 fps at 640 px, smoothing, hold-still timer), `live-outline.tsx`, `corners-view.tsx` (drag or arrow keys, 3× loupe), `flat-view.tsx` (the D5 rise-and-square animation via `matrix3d`), `scan.css`.
- **Capture** (`capture-screen.tsx`): shot → corners → straighten → flat page → tray. Gallery photos take the same path, one at a time. The auto-shutter chip is remembered (`khata-pref.ts` is now a small factory). The camera stays on between pages. A khata lock reads that page with the khata layout.
- **Server:** the migration adds `camera_path`, `camera_sha256`, `camera_width/height`, `page_corners` (fractions, EXIF-applied) and `corners_source`, all-or-none. `POST /api/documents/[id]/camera` checks the bytes like the page upload does. Delete removes both files; `seed-demo` does too (a Track B file, two lines).
- **Strings:** `scan.*`, `corners.*`, `flat.*` in bn and en. The additions beyond COPY.md Part 3 aren't in COPY.md yet. SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY has a C2 line.
- **`scripts/scan-eval.ts <out> <photos>`**: the detector on real photos, with outline overlays and the flattened page. No network.

## Decisions

- **Worker with bilinear sampling on `ImageData`, not WebGL.** It's simpler, exact, unit-tested, and has no texture-size or precision limits. Measured on desktop only; a 12 MP still peaks at about 150 MB. Revisit if F2 finds it slow on a phone.
- **`FAR_FRACTION` stays 0.35.** At that share, the page in an 8–12 MP shot gets 2.9–4.2 MP, about what the model sees (3.3 MP). Both real photos pass it.
- **The camera original goes after the readings start**, one at a time; a failure is logged and skipped. No crop (the corners are the whole photo) means the photo is the page, and there is no second copy.
- **Auto shutter:** after a shot it waits to see the page, then for it to leave, before firing again. "Retake" re-arms it at once.
- **Flat screen:** one red "পড়তে দিন" at full width, with "কোণ ঠিক করুন" and "আরেক পাতা তুলুন" as soft buttons. D5's side-by-side pair wrapped in Bangla. The corners screen uses "আবার তুলুন" (D5 copy).
- **No page found:** the dots start at the photo's corners, so a pre-cropped gallery scan is read as it is.
