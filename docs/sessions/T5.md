## Decisions I made (technical)

- **The reopen route returns the review**, so the deck goes on in place. The plan said "reload the review" (T4's note). One request instead of a page reload, and the deck can open straight on the cards rather than the overview.
- **The sheet asks the preview when it opens, not when the screen loads.** A done page costs no request until he taps. The preview is the only place the count and the closed days come from, so the sheet never guesses them from the lines on screen.
- **The ask deck opens on the deck after a reopen,** as the plan says. From the details screen, the check screen opens on its overview like any page to check: he comes from another screen, and the overview shows the parts first.
- **Both decks get the button.** The plan names the deck's done view. Prompt 4 is still the default, so most pages end on the line deck's seal, and its pages need fixing as much.
- **The details card goes above the review workspace.** In the aside it came after the whole workspace on a phone.
- **No new CSS.** The note is the deck's `dk-panel`, the errors `dk-err`, the head the fix sheet's `fx-head`.
