## What's built

- **Prompt 7** (`scriba-prompt-7`, schema 3, one call) is prompt 6 plus prompt 5's writer rules, as prompt 5 is to prompt 4.
  - The rules are adapted to blocks: a reference line never becomes a block, every box is on the page's grid, and a two-way digit gets a doubt as well as `review`.
  - It is sent as prompt 5 is: notes → reference image → "Now transcribe…" → page.
  - `getPersonalProfile()` gives prompt 5 on prompt 4 and prompt 7 on prompt 6.
  - Two-pass never applies to it, or to a khata page; both are read in one call.
  - Prompts 3–6 are unchanged word for word (checked against the previous file).
- **The khata layout on prompts 6 and 7** (`KHATA_LAYOUT_BLOCKS`, version still `khata-1`):
  - It shares prompt 4's text for print, columns and marks.
  - Blocks follow the নাম column: a name starts a block, the blank rows below belong to it, and rows above the first name are the day's sales.
  - The carry and total rows belong to the page's one party when there is one, otherwise each is its own `other` block.
  - Prompt 4's khata text is unchanged.
- **The default:** `PROMPT_VERSION` is prompt 6. Prompt 4 is `LINES_PROMPT_VERSION`.
  - Tests that read prompt 4's schema-2 fixtures now say so: `credit control` pins prompt 4, and the parse tests pass prompt 4's profile.
  - `seed-demo` parses its stored readings with their own versions.
  - `.env.example`, DATA_AND_SCHEMA and IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN are updated.
- **What the notes learn from** (`profile.ts`, `learning-data.ts`). The two fixes below matter in his own demo account:
  - **Seeded pages never reach the model** (`realReadings`, `LearningSample.seeded`). The demo seed's made-up misreads include "৬ read as ৩", the opposite of his real mix-up, and the prompt would have told the model so. The sample shop's panel still shows them. Its reference strip is now the one the model gets.
  - **A total he changed teaches nothing** (`LearningSample.sum`). A changed subtotal or closing balance gives no digit swap and is never a reference, because the total may have been written wrong. On P2, his ১৫০ → ১৪০ would have taught "৪ read as ৫", and the reference image would have shown ১৫০ labelled ১৪০. It still counts in the panel's "numbers you corrected".
- **Reference strips show the figure** (`lineRect`). The model's line boxes stop short on the right, so রহিমা জমা ২৫০ lost its ০ and চিনি ১৩০ showed only its ১. The right end now gets 15% of the line's width as padding (at least the old 10).
- **Tools:**
  - `scripts/writer-notes.ts <target> <pages…>` builds a page's notes offline, as the app would, from the other pages' saved readings against their truth. It leaves the target out and writes `private-test-data/notes/<target>.json` and `.ref.jpg`.
  - `live:extract --notes=<file>` sends a reading with those notes (prompt 7 on prompt 6).
  - `score-readings.ts --truths=<dir>` matches each file to its truth by name. It now also prints a digit score, "wrong and unflagged" amounts and the doubts, and ends with a summary per prompt.

## Decisions I made (technical)

- **Prompt 7 is a whole new version**, as prompt 5 was, not a `+notes` suffix. The run row, the cache key and evaluations tell it apart from plain prompt 6 with no parsing.
- **The notes for the measurement come from his checked truth**, not from what he confirmed. In the app they come from confirmed lines, but only P1 and P2 were confirmed, and P2's confirmed totals are the corrected ones. Truth against the saved reading is what the app learns once the pages are confirmed as written, with the two learning fixes above.
- **Corrected totals are skipped rather than judged.** Code can't tell a misread total from one written wrong: both end at the sum. A lost lesson costs less than a wrong one, and the ≥2 rule only guards the confusions, not the references.
- **The right-hand padding is 15% of the line.** That fits the shortfall on his pages (up to 6% of the page width) without reaching far into a neighbouring block.
