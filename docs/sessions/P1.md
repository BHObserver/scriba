## What's done

- **Number reader** (`src/lib/voice/number-words.ts`, 111 tests). It's pure and deterministic, and it never guesses: one number, several (the person picks), or none. It reads:
  - all Bangla words 0–99 with common spellings (ঊন/উন, missing chandrabindu, কুড়ি, তিরিশ…), with য়/ড় composed or not;
  - শো/শ/শত, হাজার, লাখ/লক্ষ and কোটি, fused or apart (একশো, বারোশো, দুহাজার);
  - দেড়, আড়াই, আধা, সাড়ে, সোয়া and পৌনে (সাড়ে তিনশো = 350, পৌনে পাঁচশো = 475);
  - টাকা … পয়সা, দশমিক/পয়েন্ট, and counters (তিনটা, দুটো);
  - digits (১,২০০, ৩.৫, ৩ হাজার ২০০), digits said one by one (এক আট শূন্য = 180), and English for the English screen.
- **Audio:** `src/lib/voice/wav.ts` encodes WAV in the browser and checks the header on the server. `recorder.ts` uses MediaRecorder, then decodes and resamples to 24 kHz mono. It stops itself at 15 s.
- **Server:**
  - `src/lib/meta/voice.ts`: multipart POST to `/v1/asr/transcribe`, push-to-talk, never retried, errors normalized, key redacted.
  - `POST /api/voice?lang=bn|en`: body is `audio/wav`. It checks auth, the WAV format and 0.3–15 s, applies the per-user limit, calls Meta, and returns `{ transcript, number }`.
  - `getVoiceConfig` (`SCRIBA_VOICE`, `META_VOICE_MODEL_ID`, limits) and `estimateVoiceCostUsd`.
  - The credit-control allow-list now names the route and `scripts/live-voice.ts`.
- **Fix sheet** (Track A file, kept small): "বলে দিন" sits beside "রাখুন" as in D2. Listening replaces the keypad (bars, "থামুন"), then a "বুঝে নিচ্ছি…" step. The heard number goes in the field, never saved. Typing cancels voice. Hook: `src/components/check/use-voice.ts`. Strings: `fx.voice` … `fx.voiceOff` (bn + en). Styles: `flow.css`.

## Decisions

- **Fixed a header that blocked the mic.** `next.config.ts` sent `Permissions-Policy: microphone=()`, which blocks getUserMedia on every page. Voice would have failed on phones too. It's now `microphone=(self)`. I found it through the Edge check.
- **Any signed-in user may use voice,** unlike extraction. The demo account must be able to show it, and a clip costs ~1/200 of a page.
  - Limits are 60 an hour and 300 a day per user, held **in memory** (no migration). Each serverless instance counts separately; see SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.
- **24 kHz,** the model's native rate per Meta's guide (16 kHz is also accepted).
- **Language bias** is `["Bengali"]` on the Bangla screen and `["English", "Bengali"]` on the English one.
- **Never guess:**
  - Several numbers become choices. Punctuation or any word between digits keeps them apart ("তিন, চার" is never 34). "one fifty" is two numbers, not 150.
  - The turmeric highlight shows only while the field holds what this clip said.
  - A fraction in a whole-taka field isn't placed.
- **Not logged:** the transcript (it's the shopkeeper's words). Only the audio length and cost are logged.
