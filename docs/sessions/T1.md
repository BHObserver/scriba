# T1: "yes" means only what was asked (3 Nov 2026)

**Status:** done, on `scriba-mvp`. Track A. 810 tests (5 new, 2 changed), typecheck and lint pass. Checked in Edge at 390×844 in Bangla and English on the dev board. No live calls, no hosted-DB change, nothing spent.

## What changed

- **One list says which answers accept lines:** `ANSWER_ACCEPTS` in `src/components/check/ask-model.ts`, typed against every question kind, so a new kind won't compile until it's listed. `answeredLines(before, after, question, answer)` applies it. The rules:

  | Rule | Kinds | Lines accepted |
  |---|---|---|
  | `lines` | `doubt`, `price` (and the prices card), `delivery`, `direction`, `memo_differs`, `block_kind` | the question's lines, and any line the answer added |
  | `changed` | `unbalanced` | only lines whose figures the answer changed ("use ৳…"), and added ones; "it's written that way" accepts none |
  | `added` | `opening` | only the "before" line the answer added |
  | `none` | `meaning`, `same_party`, `date`, `memo`, `memo_extra` | none |

  - An option that changes nothing ("later", "one by one", "I'll price each one") accepts no line, whatever its kind. Before this, "later" on an account's "before" question accepted all of the part's lines.
  - `block_kind` stays in `lines`: its card shows a chip for each line with its figure (checked in `Visual`).
  - `memo_extra` isn't named in the plan. It goes in `none` because its lines come from the memo, not the page, and its card can't change their figures. The added lines show on the quick confirm.
  - `acceptAnswered` now takes the review before and after the answer, and the answer. `ask-session.tsx` passes them from `answer` and `answerEach` (the prices card).
- **Problems he kept go on the quick confirm, not their own card.** Once "it's written that way" accepts no lines, a sum's two ends would come back one by one as "have a look" cards, asking the same thing again. `deckItems` and `acceptRest` now ignore findings he kept through an answer when deciding whether a line needs its own card. "All correct" accepts the line with that acknowledgement. The server still re-checks it at confirmation.
- **The meaning card's local option**, `line` ("একটা লাইন ভুল" / "A line is wrong", `q.meaning.line`). It's in the engine's `meaningQuestion`, so voice and the sheet see the same options. The answer route refuses it as `local`. On the card it opens the lines sheet for the part. Tapping a line opens the fix sheet, and after a fix the questions are worked out again (existing `change`). Only meaning cards whose part has lines get it.
- **Smaller fixes in `ask-session.tsx`:**
  - The lines sheet opens a line on its balance when only a balance is written, the same as the quick confirm (`figureField`). Before, it always opened the amount.
  - The quick confirm says "Check the other N lines" only when some of the part's lines aren't on it. Otherwise it says "Check the N lines of this part". Before, "other" showed whenever the part had had any question.
- Strings: `q.meaning.line` in `bn.ts` and `en.ts`, next to the other meaning keys and not at the end, so T3's appended `q.book.*` keys won't conflict. Also COPY.md Part 4.
