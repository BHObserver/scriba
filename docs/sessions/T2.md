**Status:** done, on `scriba-mvp`. Track A, with engine files. 822 tests (12 new, 1 changed), typecheck, lint and build pass. Checked in Edge at 390×844 in Bangla and English on a new dev-board page. No live calls, no hosted-DB change, nothing spent.

## What changed

- **The `split` change** (`types.ts`): `{ op: "split"; block; from; values: { kind, party?, person?, title? } }`. `applyChanges` makes the next free `bN` (one past the page's highest, `nextBlockId` in `ledger/blocks.ts`), puts it just after the block in the page's order, and moves the block's lines from `from` to its end into it by editing `values.block`.
  - The new part keeps the old part's `dateText`: it was written on the same day.
  - A rejected line stays where it was: the save path refuses edits to rejected and confirmed lines.
  - Part boxes needed no change. A part's box was already the box round its lines (`blockRegion`), so each part's box is its own lines.
  - `MAX_BLOCKS` (30) is now a constant in `review/values.ts`, used by the page schema and by both questions. No split is offered on a full page.
- **Where a split can start** (`splitLines` in `engine.ts`, shared by the change and both questions): any line below the part's first *written* line, with nothing from there down already in the khata. A "before" an answer added on top doesn't count as the first line. The Edge walk found this: after "No, nothing", চাল was no longer the first line, and splitting there would have left Karim with only the added ৳0.
- **What kind each moved line becomes** (`kindIn`, applied as `edit` changes next to the split by `splitChanges`):
  - Into day sales: a credit sale with no name becomes a cash sale (a sale on nobody's account was paid then and there).
  - Into expenses: a credit purchase with no name becomes a purchase.
  - A kind that can't be in day sales or expenses becomes `unknown`, so Scriba asks (the existing kind questions).
  - Accounts and "something else" keep the kinds. The direction question already asks about lines that point the other way.
  - Without the day-sales rule, Karim's three lines would have gone to the khata as credit sales on nobody's account. T5 needs the ৳250 in that day's cash sales.
- **Code's question `split:<block>`** (kind `split`), asked right after the part's meaning:
  - **When:** a customer's or supplier's account with ≥ 2 written totals, the lines after the first one name no one, at least one of them isn't a total or a balance, and the part has a name.
  - **Wording:** "“মোট ২৬০”-এর পরের ৩টা লাইন (৳২৫০) কি করিম-এরই?" Code counts the lines and adds them up. If one doesn't read as a number, `q.split.nosum` leaves the sum out. The `why` line: more than one total, and no name after the first.
  - **Options:** "হ্যাঁ, করিম-এরই" (no change, not asked again); "না, আলাদা বিক্রি" (→ day sales, with the detail "খাতায় নগদ বিক্রি হিসেবে উঠবে" when every line becomes cash); and a typed or spoken name, "অন্য কারো বাকি" (→ that customer's account).
  - **A supplier's account** gets "না, আলাদা কেনা" (→ expenses, as cash purchases) and "অন্য মহাজনের হিসাব".
  - Nothing is proposed: Scriba doesn't know, so he chooses. The card shows two tiles and a pill named for the typed answer.
  - Its `entries` are the first total and the lines after it, so the picture shows what the lines come after.
- **"এখান থেকে আলাদা হিসাব" / "A separate part from here"** is on the fix sheet in the ask deck, wherever a split can start (`splitHereQuestion`). It opens the answer sheet with:
  - a crop of the lines that would move;
  - "এখান থেকে নিচের ৪টা লাইন কী?";
  - দিনের বিক্রি / দোকানের খরচ / অন্য কিছু, and a name field for someone else's account.
  - Its answer goes through the answers route as `split:<block>:<line>`. `answerQuestion` works the question out again from that id (`askedFor`), so the server checks that the line is in that part and can start a split. A first line, another part's id or a line over a confirmed one is refused as `stale`. The answer is recorded on the page like any other.
- **`AnswerInput.label`** (new, optional): what typing stands for when it is one of the answers. The card's "type" pill shows it instead of "লিখে দিন". The sheet labels a split's name field `asks.in.split`.
- **The deck** (`ask-model.ts`):
  - `split` is `none` in `ANSWER_ACCEPTS`. Each part's lines come on that part's own quick confirm.
  - `acceptAnswered` takes back the acceptance of any line an answer moved to another part.
  - `ask-session.tsx` passes a card in the progress bar only when the answer is to the current card's question. A split from a sheet passes none.
- **Strings:** `q.split.*` in bn and en, next to `q.kind.*` and not at the end, so T3's appended `q.book.*` keys won't conflict. Also `asks.split` and `asks.in.split`. COPY.md Parts 4 and 5.
- **Dev board:** `/kit/check?step=asks&photo=karim` (`src/app/kit/check/karim-fixture.ts`). It is a page shaped like P1 and written for the board, not his data. The "photo" is drawn as an SVG, so the strips work without a private photo:

  - then নগদ বিক্রি: চা ২০, বিস্কুট ৪০.

## Decisions I made (technical)

- **Retyping on a split** (above). It is one rule, and it decides what reaches the khata, so the option's detail line says it on the card.
- **The new part of "someone else's account" still gets its meaning card.** He typed the name, so it's one extra tap. But it's where Scriba shows the name as it understood it (voice can mishear), and where "A line is wrong" lives for that part. I didn't write an answer for him to a card he never saw.
- **Not one transaction**, as `saveAnswer` already says: lines are saved before the page. If the page save fails after the lines moved, the lines name a part the page doesn't have yet. They show as a part with no name, and no figure is wrong. Reloading and splitting again isn't possible from there, though (the lines are no longer in the old part). Rare (a page edited elsewhere at that moment), and noted here rather than given a second page write.
