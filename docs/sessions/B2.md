## What's done

- **`/capture`** (`components/capture/capture-screen.tsx`):
  - Live camera (`getUserMedia`, rear camera, ideal 4K). Each frame is grabbed to a canvas and encoded once as a JPEG, quality 0.92, with no EXIF. That JPEG is the stored original.
  - Guide frame, flash, tray of up to 8 pages (turn, remove), gallery (multiple files).
  - No camera (a laptop, or permission refused): "open the phone camera" (`capture="environment"`) and the gallery.
  - "Read (n)" runs the sideways check, then uploads every original (progress shown as step 1), starts every reading, and opens `/check/<first>?pages=<ids>`.
- **Reading queue** (`reading-queue.tsx`):
  - A provider in the `(app)` layout. It runs the paid analyze calls two at a time, so they survive moving between `/capture` and `/check`.
  - One request id per tap, `use_cache`, never retried.
  - It also keeps the turns each page was sent with.
- **`/check/[documentId]`** (`components/check/check-screen.tsx`) picks one state:
  - reading (queue, or a pending run polled every 4 s);
  - the checking step (700 ms, only when the reading landed while watching);
  - the reveal;
  - "nothing to check";
  - a problem screen;
  - "not read yet": turn the photo, then one tap reads it.
  - Opening the page never sends anything.
- **Reveal** (`reveal-view.tsx`):
  - Boxes draw at a 60 ms stagger, then settle as the validator runs in the browser over the page's current values.
  - "!" badges sit outside the multiply blend.
  - Chips: add up / to look at / read but not checked.
  - The primary button goes to the details page until B3, and to "See page N" when there are more pages.
- **Problems:** `ProblemView` covers the D4 states plus `sendFailed` and `notAllowed`. `problems.ts` maps analyze outcomes and stored runs onto them.
- **Shell:** the camera tab goes to `/capture`; the tab bar hides on `/capture` and `/check/*` (`isFlowRoute`). Upload helpers are shared with the old `UploadPanel`.
- **Dev only:**
  - `/kit/check?step=…` (add `&photo=S01.jpg` for another photo);
  - `/kit/photo/[name]`, which serves `private-test-data` (404 in production);
  - `/kit/*` is public outside production (the proxy now matches the prefix).

## Decisions

- **Line states (`check/line-state.ts`; B3 must reuse it so the deck and the reveal agree):**
  - `look`: a failure, or a model flag.
  - `ok`: a passing check covers the line.
  - `plain`: nothing verified it, so the line is never claimed as checked.
  - A failed sum is shown on the written figures it compares, not on every summand: the total for a written total, the two ends for the balance and running balance. That's the prototype's design.
  - On S04 this gives 3 ok, 2 look and 1 plain (the repayment).
- **Sideways check:** a page wider than tall (after turns, at the browser-shown size) asks "is it upright?". There is no detector. Wide snippets get asked once, and he taps "it's upright".
- **Honest errors:** an upload failure says "couldn't send", not "the reading service didn't answer". A timed-out request that Meta may have processed shows "waiting" with "check again", never a resend. 403 shows `notAllowed`.
- **Server-side staleness:** a pending run older than `stalePendingMs` shows "waiting". This is computed in a loader outside the component, because the purity lint rejects `Date.now()` in render.
