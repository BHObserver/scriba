## Decisions

- **Sleeker, not a new direction.** There are no hairlines on the new cards, only a three-layer shadow and soft fills. The question is 26 px. Each card has one red action; everything else is a soft pill. Red still means the khata and turmeric still means "needs a look".
- **Block tones**: a visit or supplier block is blue and day sales are green. The tone appears on the region, the tag, the avatar and the progress segments. A finished block gets a small red stamp "মিলেছে"; the big seal is still only for the page.
- **Deck order**: page questions first, then block by block: that block's questions, a quick confirm of its other lines, then its stamp. The page seal comes last. One question per card.
- **Answer types:**
  - Confirm: a red button that states the answer, plus swipe right.
  - Choose: one tap answers; back undoes it.
  - Type: the keypad sheet.
  - Voice: in the same sheet; the parsed value goes into the field and is never auto-saved.
  - Swipe works only on yes-cards.
- **A missing price is a gap, never a zero.** It shows as a dashed turmeric pill. The visit's "owed now" shows the known part plus the gap until every item is priced. "Later" leaves the gap open; then that block gets no stamp, the page gets no seal, and the unpriced goods stay unconfirmed.
- **Code-computed context in questions.** "৳৯০ = ৬টা, ৳১৫ করে" against "৳১০ = ৳১.৬৭ করে", and "your khata also shows ৳২,৮০০ for them". This helps him judge without Scriba guessing.
- **Flatten = homography.** `quadMatrix` (Heckbert square-to-quad as CSS `matrix3d`) both draws the photographed page and animates the flatten, so the outline is exact. C2's flatten is the inverse of the same map.
- **The scanner coaches distance.** When the page is less than about 35 % of the frame, it says "come closer" and takes no auto shot. That was the 2 Nov failure.

## Gotchas

- `write_files` sends Bangla as `\u` escapes, which is expensive. To fix a file, download it with the serve URL, edit it with `sed`, and re-upload. Generate COPY.md from `T` with a script.
- The six review fixes sit in the HTML `<style>` (`:has()` for card spacing, among others). Fold them into `scan.css` when porting.
- The voice step auto-clicks the first price and then the mic (`scan-app.jsx`). That is for the board only.
