## Decisions

- Problems are marked with turmeric, not red; red means the khata (identity, primary action, seal).
- Fonts: Anek Bangla for everything; Noto Serif Bengali only in the red cover and the seal. The handwriting font (Atma) is for design mocks only; the app shows real photo crops.
- Icons: `lucide-react` (B1 adds it).
- The red cover appears only on Today and Close the day; every other screen has the 7 px binding band.
