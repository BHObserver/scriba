## Decisions

- **Today** follows the D1 canvas (1c cover + 1b body):
  - The hero is the drawer cash ৳৪,৩২০, with the equation written under it.
  - Two tiles: বিক্রি, with a thin cash/credit bar, and টাকা গেছে (কেনা, মহাজন, খরচ).
  - **দিনের কথা** holds up to 3 sentences, picked in priority order: unchecked page, then sales split, then the top dues risk, then the week trend.
  - Then the দিনের হিসাব মেলান button, the seven-day bars, and the dues link. After closing, the button becomes a sealed row.
- **Every number is a button.** It opens a sheet listing its lines: handwriting, then the reading, then type · page, with a footer that says code added them. The drawer figure opens a receipt instead, and each receipt row drills further, with a back link.
- **Chart colours.** The new token `--blue-2: oklch(0.70 0.11 262)` is the second step after `--blue`. It is used for the credit share and for the earlier days in the bars. The dataviz validator passes the pair. Its contrast warning is covered by legend values and an sr-only table.
- **Bars.** One series; today is solid `--blue`, earlier days are lighter, with a hairline at the average. Only today's value is labelled; tapping or hovering a bar shows its value.
- **Close the day:**
  - Review shows the pages (seal, tick or "not checked yet") and the breakdown.
  - The count sheet uses the kit keypad.
  - **Match:** a card with the seal (vibrate 30), then "দিন বন্ধ করুন". The counted cash becomes tomorrow's opening cash.
  - **Gap:** a turmeric card saying "৳৫০ কম আছে" with three possible reasons. The red button says exactly what it does: "৳৫০ কম লিখে দিন বন্ধ করুন".
  - An unchecked page never enters any figure, and the panel says so.
- **Risk rules** (proposals for E2, each with a reason line):
  - No payment for ≥ 30 days (customers) or ≥ 21 days (suppliers).
  - Balance up three weeks running, using weekly balances.
  - One credit ≥ 3× the party's usual and ≥ ৳১,০০০. "Usual" is the median of their credits older than 7 days.
  - Sort order: rule rank, then days, then balance. Parties with no risk show "শেষ জমা …".
- **Party ledger.** A stitched hero shows the balance, the reasons, the last payment and the oldest unpaid credit (FIFO). Below it, a timeline, newest first: date, handwriting, verb + reading, ± amount, and the running balance after it. A line with no photo shows "ছবি নেই · নমুনা লাইন" (E3's sample records).
- **Reminder:**
  - The message uses `call` (how he addresses them: রহিম ভাই, সুমাইয়া আপা), the shop name and the last payment date.
  - The attachment is the unpaid lines that have photos, as one image.
  - Buttons: কপি করুন and পাঠান (Web Share). The sheet says nothing is sent until he picks an app.
- **Ask Scriba:**
  - The answer card shows the number, one sentence, and the proving lines (2 shown, then "আরও"), or a day list for period questions.
  - Footer: "হিসাব করেছে কোড … AI শুধু প্রশ্নটা বুঝেছে।"
  - A question it can't map gets "এই প্রশ্নের উত্তর খাতায় নেই, তাই Scriba আন্দাজে কিছু বলবে না।" plus suggestions.
  - Shapes used: `party_due`, `sales_week`, `oldest_due`, `cash_today`.
- **Sample badge** "নমুনা দোকান" appears on every screen: top right of the cover, or in the title bar.
- **Bangla case endings** come from `poss`/`loc` in `shop.js`: a word ending in a vowel sign takes র/য়, a consonant takes ের/ে ("সেপ্টেম্বরের", "স্টোরে"). E2's note templates need the same rule.

## Gotchas

- Don't name a screen element `.cover`. `.phone.cover` matches it too. The header class is `.cloth`.
- The status bar is transparent over the cloth, so scrolled content passes under it. In the app, set the PWA `theme-color` to the cloth red on Today and Close, and to paper elsewhere.
- Tap-through script pattern: `scratchpad/flow.mjs` (Playwright `getByRole("button", { name })` plus `.key` clicks). Tiling screenshots with sharp needs `createRequire` from the repo's `package.json`.
