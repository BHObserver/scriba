## What changed

### The book (`src/lib/insights/accounts.ts`)

- **A later "before" is the page's statement of the due.** A party's first `opening_balance` is still its starting point (`start`). A later one:
  - **equal** to the running balance: nothing changes, and no timeline line;
  - **different:** a new entry direction, `adjust`, whose `amountPoisha` is the signed difference. The balance becomes the written figure.
- **How an adjustment counts** (the plan's suggestion, tested):
  - **Down:** clears the oldest credits first, like a payment (FIFO), but it isn't a payment. `lastPayment` skips it, so "no payment for N days" still flags.
  - **Up:** owed like a credit, after the credits before it. It's in `unpaidRecordIds`, so the reminder's picture can show the "before" line.
  - **Either way:** never a credit he gave, so it is neither the unusual credit nor part of the "usual" one (`big_credit`). Weekly balances include it: the balance did change.
- **`dueBefore(book, party, page)`:** the due just before a page, in khata order (day, then photo time, as `compareRecords`), leaving out the page's own records (a page checked again). Merged spellings are followed to their party.
- **Karim, as the plan said:** the real dev book gives ৳60, not ৳310. P1's lines come to ৳510, P2's typed "before ৳260" becomes an adjustment of −৳250, then the ৳200 payment. ## Decisions I made (technical)

- **An adjustment is its own direction with a signed amount**, as the plan wrote it, rather than an "up" or "down" with a flag. The compiler then found every place that reads a direction (the timeline's verbs), and no code can count an adjustment as a payment or a credit by accident.
- **"Before this page", not "as of the page's day":** on a day with two pages, the second page's "before" is what the first left. The page's own records are left out, so a page checked again (T4's reopen) compares with the khata without itself.
- **The khata's figure goes in as the answer's line** ("yes" adds a line with ৳260). The line is then in the page's sums, and the book sees it equal and adds nothing. No special case on confirm.
- **`book_differs` proposes nothing.** A misread digit and a khata that's wrong look the same to code. He looks at the strip.
