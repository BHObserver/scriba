## What's done

- **Reading** (`src/lib/memo/`): prompt `scriba-memo-1` and schema `scriba-memo-1` (strict JSON Schema plus zod, kept in agreement by a test). The model copies the letterhead's company, the rep, the memo's number and date, one line per row of goods (item, qty, unit, rate, amount, box) and the totals (subtotal, discount, total, previous due, paid, due), every figure as written. `parseMemoResponse` turns figures into poisha and checks the memo's own sums: qty × rate per line (to the taka, as memos round), and the lines against the written subtotal and total less any discount (`check.agrees`).
- **One call, the page's credit rules** (`pipeline.ts`): a memo is a document of kind `memo`, read through the same analyze route and `analyzeDocument`. So it gets the cache, the request id, one pending run per key, the rate limits (1 call) and the checksum, never with his notes, a khata layout or two passes. The single-call path is shared by pages and memos (`callOnce`, `persist`). A memo's reading is its run's `result` (`completeMemoRun`): no review rows, no findings, never confirmed.
- **Storage** (migration): `ledger_documents.kind` (`page` | `memo`) and `memo_for` (its page, same owner, cascade on delete). A trigger keeps a memo's page a page. Users can't set either column. `POST /api/documents` takes `memoFor`: the page must be his and a page; at most 6 memos per page; a photo already stored as a page can't come back as a memo, or the other way round. Deleting a page removes its memos' photos too. The money screens, the photo list and the evaluations list pages only.
- **Matching** (`src/lib/memo/match.ts`, pure): a memo belongs to the visit whose company or rep it names (`romanize`, so প্রাণ meets "PRAN Foods Ltd." and কামাল meets "Kamal"). Items are compared word by word without the company's name, sizes and pack words, with a soft and a hard "c" and a plural "s" (জুস ↔ "Frooto Mango Juice 250ml", Buiscite ↔ "Potato Biscuit", Conacor ↔ "Chanachur"). A pair is sure only when each side is the other's single best match; the quantity breaks ties; the last goods line and memo line left are paired only when their quantities agree.
- **Questions** (`engine.ts`, new kinds `memo`, `memo_extra`, `memo_differs`; `QuestionInput.memos`; `Question.memo` names the memo and its lines for the card):
  - `memo:<memo doc>`: asked only when the memo names no visit (or several). Options `block:<id>` (“Yes, it's theirs”), `no`, later. Nothing is proposed. His answer links it.
  - `delivery`: when every unpriced good has a sure, confidently read memo line and the memo's sums agree, one card proposes the memo's prices, one edit per line (amount, and the rate when he wrote the quantity the memo charges for). After a discount, the memo's net total is proposed as one delivery line instead (U2's delivery total), but only when the memo's lines are exactly these goods. Otherwise the plain question is asked, with a `why` that says what the memo has.
  - `price`: the same, line by line, after “I'll price each one”. Unsure or ambiguous lines are offered as options, never proposed.
  - `memo_extra` / `memo_extras`: priced memo lines that none of the visit's goods may be. Add (a priced goods line in the block), leave out, or later; two or more are one question with “one by one”.
  - `memo_differs:<row>`: a priced goods line, the visit's “before” or its payment that the memo gives differently. Page or memo; nothing proposed.
  - `why` lines: “With these prices you owe them ৳3,000, the same due as on the memo.” (ticked: code worked out the page's due with the proposed prices and it equals the memo's printed due), else “The memo's lines add up to its total.” (ticked). On the visit's `meaning` question: “The memo shows the same: ৳2,800 before and ৳1,500 paid.” (ticked).
- **Text**: 40 `q.memo.*` keys in `bn.ts` and `en.ts`; COPY.md Part 4 lists them (generated from the code).
- **`bun run live:memo`** (`scripts/live-memo.ts`): one memo photo in one call, or a saved memo response read again for free; with `--page=<saved page reading>` it prints the questions and what one tap on each proposal does.

## Decisions (reasons in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN)

- **A memo is a document (one additive migration), not JSON on the page.** It needs a run to be read (every paid call is a run) and runs belong to a document. Page values are user-writable, so a memo's path or checksum can't live there. A `kind` keeps it out of every page list.
- **The memo isn't reviewed.** A misread memo price is corrected on the page line (he types the price), which is what gets confirmed. The memo stays the model's reading, kept as evidence.
- **Propose only what three things support**: a sure pair, an amount the model was sure of that multiplies out, and sums that don't contradict each other. Anything less is offered, not proposed. A discount means line prices aren't what he owes, so only the net total is proposed then.
- **One memo per visit**: the first that is its. A second memo of the same company asks which visit it is for, among the visits left.
- **Pages without blocks get no memo questions**: memos link to visit blocks (prompt 6).
