## What's done

- **Engine (`src/lib/ask/`):**
  - `query.ts`: the query shape (intent, party/item picked from the shop's list or null, the words said, a period key with days/day/month, kinds) and its strict JSON Schema. Also the shop vocabulary (names by last activity, items from sale/purchase/cost lines only) and party matching. Matching handles case endings করিমের/করিমকে/আপার, titles, merged spellings and part of a name.
  - `period.ts`: code turns period keys into business dates. A date that doesn't exist (৩১ নভেম্বর) is null, never a guess.
  - `answer.ts`: due, total dues, risky dues, sales (by kind or item), money out (by kind or item), compare, drawer, pick (two people with one name), not found, unknown. Every figure carries its record ids.
  - `text.ts`: one sentence from `ask.*` templates (bn + en), period phrases, and suggestions.
- **Model:** `src/lib/meta/ask.ts`, `prompt.ts` (`scriba-ask-1`), `getAskConfig` (`SCRIBA_ASK`, `META_ASK_MODEL_ID`, `SCRIBA_ASK_REASONING_EFFORT`, limits). One call per question, never retried, `store: false`.
- **`POST /api/ask`:**
  - `{ question }` runs the model, then code. `{ query }` (a suggestion) runs code only.
  - It returns the answer, the proof ids newest first (300 max) and their count, plus `byModel`.
  - Limits are 20 an hour and 60 a day per user, held in memory. The question isn't logged.
- **UI** (a Track A change, kept to new files plus small hooks):
  - `src/components/ask/` holds the sheet and the pill, styled from D3's `proto/money.css`. The pill sits in Today's note card (alone when there's no note) and on History under the search.
  - The answer card shows the number, one sentence, the dates covered, then the parts, risky rows or compare rows, and the proof lines with handwriting (2, then 50). Under it come "how this was worked out" and an open-party or open-dues link.
  - The footer drops "AI শুধু প্রশ্নটা বুঝেছে" for suggestions, because no AI was involved.
  - `useVoice` now also passes its transcript to the callback (the fix sheet ignores it).
- `demoBookInput(now)` moved from `shop.test.ts` into `src/lib/shop/demo.ts` for the live script.

## Decisions

- **The model can't produce a number or a stranger.** The shape has no amount field, and names and items are an `enum` of the shop's own list. Periods are keys; code works out the dates and shows them on the card ("২৬ অক্টোবর থেকে ১ নভেম্বর").
- **What goes to Meta:** the question plus names and item names only. See SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.
- **Suggestions cost nothing.** Each one carries its shape. The first names the riskiest customer.
- **"This week" means the last 7 days**, the same window as Today's week bars and the note.
- **Money out with an item** counts stock bought on credit too. Plain "money out" is cash out, as on Today.
- **Limits were lowered from my first guess** after the live run showed ~$0.013 a question. That's mostly reasoning tokens at effort `low`; `minimal` is untested.
