# V1: measure Bangla speech, part 1: the measuring kit (3 Nov 2026)

**Status:** part 1 done, on `scriba-mvp`. The measurement (part 2) waits for his recordings: none exist yet. 869 tests (11 new), typecheck, lint and build pass. No paid call, $0.

## What's ready

- **`bun run live:voice <folder>`** sends every clip in a folder, in name order (`scripts/live-voice.ts`). One clip still works as before.
  - **Any phone audio** (m4a, ogg, mp3, wav…). ffmpeg converts it to 24 kHz mono PCM16, as the app's recorder does, into `<folder>/sent/`. A clip already in that format is sent as is.
  - **`P1-b3-quiet.m4a`** is part b3 of `private-test-data/ground-truth/P1.json`, quiet take. Any other name is transcribed, not scored.
  - **Over 15 s** is still sent (up to 2 min) and marked. How long a part takes is a finding.
  - **Refuses to start** above `--max-usd` (default $0.10, V1's budget). 30 clips of ~10 s cost about $0.015.
  - **Saves** the transcripts to `<folder>/results.<time>.private.json`. **`--rescore=<file>`** scores them again for free, so number-words can be improved against real transcripts.
  - **`--dry-run`** converts and checks the clips, shows what each part should carry, and sends nothing.
- **`src/lib/evaluation/spoken-part.ts`** (pure, tested):
  - `expectedPart(truth, block)`: the part's written figures in page order, each with its role (amount, total, before, paid, qty), its names and items, and the marker groups it calls for (total, before, paid, credit, visit).
  - `scorePart(expected, transcript)`: the numbers `readSpokenNumbers` finds, each figure heard or missed, extra numbers, each word exact, near or missed, and the markers said.
  - Per clip: money, counts, words. Per take: the same summed, markers said where the part has one, round trip.
- **Checked at $0:** a dry run on synthetic clips (m4a, stereo 44.1 kHz WAV, a 17 s ogg, an unnamed WAV), and a re-score of hand-written transcripts against the real P1–P3 truth.
- `private-test-data/voice/` exists (git-ignored) for the clips.

## Decisions (technical)

- **Phone recordings, not an in-app recorder.** The app keeps no clip, and in the shop the phone's recorder is what he has. Conversion matches the app's format. The app also asks the browser for noise suppression; the phone does its own. If noise is the problem, part 2 sends a few clips through the fix sheet to compare.
- **Figures pair in order** (longest common sequence). A figure said out of order counts as missed plus extra: V2 must pair voice with ink by order too.
- **Near words:** the same after folding spellings transcripts swap (ঁ, nukta, ণ/ন, ী/ি, ূ/ু, শ/ষ/স), or one letter off for words of 5+ letters. Short words must match exactly: চাল and ডাল are one letter apart.
- **Go/no-go, set before the numbers come** (proposed, the user may change it):
  - quiet: ≥ 90% of money figures heard;
  - noise: ≥ 80%;
  - median round trip ≤ 3 s.
  - Names and items are reported, not gated: V2 can match them against the page's reading.
